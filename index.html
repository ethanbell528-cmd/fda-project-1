Methodology

Where the data comes from and how every number is computed

What one row is

The core data set is a panel with one row per team per game: every game appears twice, once from each team's side, with the date, season, sport, team, opponent, home/away/neutral, points (or runs or goals) for and against, the result, and, where a betting market existed, the line, the total, the moneyline or 1X2 odds and the outcome against them. The time column is the date and season; the group columns are sport and team. There is one file per sport (data/nfl.csv, data/nba.csv, data/mlb.csv, data/nhl.csv, data/epl.csv) with the same 24 columns. Player data lives in separate files: per-game rows for recent seasons (data/<sport>_players.csv) and season totals for the full span (data/<sport>_player_seasons.csv).

  • MLB 2026 games and box scores: MLB Stats API, statsapi.mlb.com; player id crosswalk from the Chadwick Bureau register, github.com/chadwickbureau/register.
  • NHL games, skater and goalie game logs: NHL Stats API, api.nhle.com/stats/rest/en/game.
  • EPL results, 1X2, Asian handicap and over/under odds: Football-Data.co.uk, www.football-data.co.uk/englandm.php.
  • EPL player goals and assists: Fantasy Premier League history compiled by vaastav, github.com/vaastav/Fantasy-Premier-League (FPL scoring rules, not official Opta records).
  • Live scores and fallback odds: ESPN's public scoreboard feed (site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/…/scoreboard); live odds, when a key is configured, from The Odds API. Both are read with HTTP GET only.
  • Live player prop lines: the full-game over/under player lines in ESPN's public odds feed (sports.core.api.espn.com/…/odds/<provider>/propBets), from the sportsbook ESPN lists, currently DraftKings. The dashboard shows them next to the model's player projections. For small count lines, such as 0.5 hits or 1.5 strikeouts, the model's chance of going over is P(X ≥ line rounded up), with X Poisson-distributed around the projection. For other stats it shows the projection minus the line. No free archive of historical prop lines exists, so these comparisons are not backtested and carry no edge flag.

Rows dropped and why

How every rate, ratio and average is computed

The report and the dashboard use exactly the same definitions:

  • Win % = rows with result W ÷ all rows (ties and draws count as non-wins).
  • Cover % = cover ÷ (cover + miss), with pushes and games without a line left out. A team covers when margin + line > 0, where line is the team's own handicap (negative = favored). For EPL Asian handicaps, a quarter line that would pay a half-win is counted as a cover.
  • Over % = over ÷ (over + under), with pushes left out. EPL's total line is 2.5 goals, set only for matches where the source has over/under 2.5 odds.
  • Favorite = rows with favorite = 1: the team with the negative line, or, when there is no line, the team with the higher no-vig implied win probability. NBA seasons from 2022-23 have spreads but no moneylines, so the favorite is defined by the spread alone there.
  • Home win % = win % over rows with home_away = H; neutral-site games (Super Bowls, international games, the 2020 NBA bubble) are excluded.
  • Average total = mean of total, the two teams' combined score, over one row per game. The scoring index divides each season's average by the average over the sport's first ten seasons and multiplies by 100.
  • Days of rest = days between a team's game and its previous game in the same regular season.
  • No-vig implied probability. For American odds A, the raw probability is −A ÷ (−A + 100) when A < 0 and 100 ÷ (A + 100) when A > 0. The two sides are divided by their sum so they add to 1. For EPL 1X2 decimal odds d, the raw probability is 1 ÷ d, normalised over home, draw and away.
  • Fair odds from a model probability p. American: −100·p ÷ (1 − p) when p ≥ 0.5, otherwise 100·(1 − p) ÷ p. Decimal (EPL): 1 ÷ p.
  • Log loss = −mean(y·ln p + (1 − y)·ln(1 − p)) and Brier score = mean((p − y)²), where y = 1 if the team won. Lower is better for both.
  • Return at −110 = (wins × 100 − losses × 110) ÷ (bets × 110), pushes returned; this is the standard price of a spread bet and needs a 52.4% win rate to break even.

Honest limits

  • Betting lines before 2007-08 (NBA, NHL) and 2010 (MLB) do not exist in any free archive, so those seasons show “n/a” for line fields. MLB lines stop after 2021 and NHL lines after part of 2022-23 because no free archive covers later seasons.
  • Only EPL from 2019-20 has odds explicitly recorded at kick-off. NFL lines are the sources' pre-game reference lines. The NBA, NHL and MLB archives label their lines as closing lines, but that cannot be independently verified. EPL odds before 2019-20 are pre-match snapshots.
  • Player-game stats start in 1999 (NFL), 2001-02 (NBA) and 2016-17 (EPL). MLB and NHL player data cover the full span.
  • The NHL treats Utah (2024-25 onward) as a new franchise, so Arizona (ARI) and Utah (UTA) appear as separate teams.
  • NHL scores are as published: a shootout win counts as a one-goal win.
